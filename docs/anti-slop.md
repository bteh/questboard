# Questboard never applies for you

This is a design decision, not a missing feature.

Application volume in some job categories grew nine-fold between 2022
and 2025, almost all of it machine-fired. Employers responded the way
anyone would: AI screening on their side, "AI-free interview" policies,
and a growing reflex to bin anything that smells automated. On the
receiving end, a POST fired by a platform's servers is indistinguishable
from spray-and-pray, no matter how carefully the platform capped it or
how sincerely the user confirmed it. One blocked platform poisons every
application it ever touched.

So the line is structural:

**Questboard prepares; you send.** The application kit detects the ATS,
prefills every field it can (name, contact, cover letter, resume), and
hands you the exact page to act on. The click is yours. On the
receiving end you are a person who applied fast and well-prepared,
because that is what you are.

The same line runs through the whole product:

- Every posting on the board links to the place you act, never to a
  page about it, and comes down on its own when the source stops
  listing it.
- Pay renders only as the source stated it. Ceilings say they are
  ceilings. No estimates, ever.
- Materials are drafts in your voice for you to edit and own, not
  boilerplate fired in your name.
- The log records what YOU did. "Applied" means you applied.

The fastest way to lose a hiring market is to help flood it. The way to
win one is to help people show up early, real, and ready. That is the
bet.
