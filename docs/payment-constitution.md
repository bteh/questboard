# The payment constitution

One rule, stated once, so nobody ever has to wonder:

**Questboard never accepts payment to list, rank, recommend, or grant
access to people. Listed parties never pay. Seekers may, whether they
are a person or a studio.**

What that means in practice:

- No employer, grant maker, conference, accelerator, research firm,
  bank, or casting company can pay to be on the board, to rank higher
  on it, or to reach the people using it. There is no sales channel for
  this; the answer is no before the email is opened.
- Every quest is pinned because it cleared the same bars as every other
  quest (real ask, real place to act, real counterparty, real date,
  pay only as stated), never because someone paid.
- The board itself is free, forever, for everyone.
- The only money Questboard ever takes is from the SEEKER side, for
  depth on top of the free board (one-time or seasonal passes, never
  subscriptions dressed as passes, never access to listings). A studio
  hunting clients is a seeker; a conference hunting speakers is a
  listed party. The role decides, not the company size.
- Ranking is fit and freshness. There is no boost product, and there
  never will be one.

Why this is load-bearing: a board that takes listing money eventually
shows you what paid to be shown. The entire product is the promise that
what you see is what is real. Selling placement would spend the only
thing Questboard has.

Adopted 2026-07-10 (the strategy council's product-writer seat, adopted
verbatim by all five seats); published with the B2B pilot.
