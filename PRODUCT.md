# PRODUCT.md, Questboard

## Register

product (app UI is the default register). Landing and marketing surfaces run in the brand register per task: the landing page's job is to sell the board, the app's job is to work.

## What it is

One board for every side quest. Real paid opportunities pulled live from real sources: career jobs, TV audience seats, casting calls, paid studies, beginner camera gigs, party quests with friends. Pay is shown only when the source states it. Every date is the true post date. Every card ends in one primary link to the source. The career vertical is the deep spine: resume fit, scoring, tracked applications, offer support.

## Users

- Side-gig hunters. Discovery happens on the phone (dead hours at work, the couch), completion happens on a laptop. Non-technical; they know ChatGPT exists but will never learn what a model or a download-into-browser is.
- Job seekers who want their resume matched against real postings without LinkedIn theater.
- Today a single local-first user; a hosted web app is the destination. The desktop wrap and a thin phone client come later.

## Purpose

Find a quest worth an evening, understand the pay and requirements honestly, and land it. For career: match jobs to the resume, close the gaps, track everything in a log that counts only what actually paid out.

## Brand personality

A plainspoken field guide. Honest, warm, unhurried. The world is a trade paper: postal stamps, ledgers, postmarks, a masthead. It says less and shows receipts.

## Anti-references (never ship these)

- AI-slop tells: em dashes, triads, buzzwords, sparkle icons, gradient text, hero-metric blocks, the cream-SaaS template look, eyebrow kickers on every section
- Gamification: XP, streaks, progress bars, leaderboards, confetti
- Dishonesty: invented pay figures, fit percentages, fake scarcity or urgency, dark patterns, ghost-job silence
- Subscription mechanics: nags, paywalled essentials, lifetime-deal regret; the model is free core plus one-time passes
- LinkedIn-speak in user-facing copy ("pipeline", "leverage", "kit")
- Momentum's sins: forced accounts, upgrade pop-ups

## Accessibility

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all UI text (chosen for legibility, it is the brand). WCAG AA contrast. Plain words for a non-technical reader. Keyboard reachable, prefers-reduced-motion renders final states.

## Strategic design principles

1. Answer before ask: value renders before anything is requested (the board works before setup, the AI answers before its download asks).
2. Honesty is structural: only stated pay, true post dates, method lines on machine-written text, no number the source did not state.
3. The machinery stays invisible: features are verbs; the word AI appears once, in Settings, where the Remove button lives.
4. Free core forever; one-time passes at the moments the board pays off.
5. One primary source link per card; the board never traps.
